# Google Play Store listing copy — Wheel Rush

Copy-paste these directly into Play Console → your app → Store presence → Main store listing.

## App name (30 char max)
```
Wheel Rush
```

## Short description (80 char max)
```
Dodge, roll, collect coins. No ads, no purchases — just a quick race.
```

## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Jump on your wheel and race down three lanes packed with rocks, rival
racers, and coins. Swipe or tap left and right — that's the whole
control scheme. Simple to pick up, hard to put down.

🏁 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the left or right side of the screen to switch lanes
• Dodge rocks and rival racers
• Grab coins for extra score
• Speed increases the longer you survive — how far can you go?

🎮 WHY WHEEL RUSH
• Zero ads. Zero interruptions.
• Zero in-app purchases. Nothing to buy, ever.
• One-touch controls that work with your thumb, one-handed
• A few seconds to load, a few seconds to understand, minutes to master
• Your best score is saved on your device — beat it next time

No account needed. No permissions beyond what makes the app run. Just
open it and go.
```

## Content rating questionnaire
- No violence, no user-generated content, no in-app purchases, no data
  collection beyond local device storage → should land at "Everyone" / PEGI 3
  once you fill out Play Console's rating questionnaire.

## Privacy policy
Play Console requires a privacy policy URL even for an app this simple.
Host `privacy-policy.html` (in this folder) anywhere public — a GitHub
Pages page, or a route on your existing minihub domain/worker — and
paste that URL into Play Console → App content → Privacy policy.

## Screenshots
You'll need at least 2 phone screenshots (Play Console requirement).
Easiest path: build the debug APK, run it in an emulator or your own
phone, and just screenshot the start screen + a mid-run screen. The
in-game HUD is already clean and legible at any density.
