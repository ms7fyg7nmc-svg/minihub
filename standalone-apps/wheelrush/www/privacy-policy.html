Wheel Rush — Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Wheel Rush is a single-player game. It does not require an account, does not connect to any server to play, and does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

Your best score is saved locally on your device only (using standard on-device storage), so it's remembered between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party.

What the app does NOT do

  • No account creation, no sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files

Third-party links

The game-over screen includes an optional button linking to our Telegram channel for more free games. Tapping it opens Telegram (or your browser) and is subject to Telegram's own privacy policy — we don't receive any information about whether you tap it.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever changes to add features that involve data collection, this page will be updated accordingly before that update is released.
